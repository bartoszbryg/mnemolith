What Recallith stores

  • A local SQLite database
  • Conversation text encrypted with AES-256-GCM; key in your OS keychain
  • Page URLs and search-index content stored unencrypted on your device
  • Local embeddings for semantic search

What Recallith never does

  • Send captured data to our server
  • Upload captured memories to Recallith servers
  • Train on your activity
  • Share data with advertisers
Data location: your user profile's .recallith folder. Conversation text is encrypted; URLs, index data, and logs are not. Account and plan checks use Supabase. Cloud AI mode sends your question and retrieved context to the selected provider.
